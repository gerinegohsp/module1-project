📊 How to Read the Dashboard Charts

Q1: Salary Benchmark for Role X
- Look at the bar/box plot by position level or industry.
- Median = typical salary offered.
- P25/P75 = lower and upper ranges.
- Use filters (Industry filter, Year) to narrow to a role.
- Agency toggle if agenciesvs direct employeers offer different pay.
- Answer: Offer around the median; higher if you want to attract talent quickly.

Q2: Hard-to-Fill Roles
- Scatter plot: X = repost count, Y = applications per vacancy.
- Bottom-right quadrant = hard to fill (many reposts, few applications).
- Each dot = a role; hover for details.
- Industry filter → focus on one industry to reduce scatter clutter.
- Year filter → compare difficulty across years.
- Agency toggle → check if agency-posted jobs are harder to fill.
- Answer: These roles need higher salaries, better ads, or broader sourcing.

Q3: Most Demand
- Horizontal bar chart: Top 10 industries by vacancies.
- Longer bars = industries with more open positions.
- Position level bars show demand by seniority.
- Year filter → see demand trends by year.
- Industry filter not needed (chart already shows top 10 industries).
- Agency toggle → compare demand between agency vs direct postings.
- Answer: These industries/levels are hiring most aggressively.

Q4: Selective Hiring
- Same scatter plot as Q2.
- Top-left quadrant = selective hiring (low reposts, high applications).
- Industry filter → zoom into one sector.
- Year filter → see if selective hiring shifts over time.
- Agency toggle → check if agencies attract more applicants per vacancy.
- Answer: You can be choosy here — more applicants than vacancies.

Q5: When to Post Jobs
- Line chart: Blue = postings, Orange = avg applications.
- Peaks in postings = busy hiring months.
- Peaks in applications = months when candidates are most active.
- Year filter → focus on specific year’s monthly trend.
- Industry filter → compare posting/application trends within one sector.
- Agency toggle → see if agencies vs direct employers post at different times.
- Answer: Post during months where applications peak to maximize reach.

Q6: Agency vs Direct Employer
- Bar chart comparing vacancies posted by agencies vs direct employers.
- Taller bar = more postings.
- Sidebar toggle lets you filter by agency/direct.
- Agency toggle → this is the main filter (All, Agency, Direct).
- Industry filter → compare agency vs direct within one sector.
- Year filter → see if agency vs direct dominance changes by year.
- Answer: Shows whether agencies or direct employers dominate hiring.
